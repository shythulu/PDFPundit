What the research found so far

  • Trust is the hard part, not coverage. The usual ways to decide a repair is right are weaker than the design assumed. Salvaged stream prefixes are usually wrong, and a checksum match isn't unique.
  • Engines disagree on basic repair rules. They differ on which duplicate object wins and what happens to truncated streams. Attackers use those differences, so a forensic repair must report its choices.
  • Real damage with known originals exists. Truncated web-crawl captures pair with later full downloads. That lets fidelity be scored on real damage.
  • The reference corpus doesn't match its paper. REPDF's released generator differs from the published one, so replication uses the corpus as released.
