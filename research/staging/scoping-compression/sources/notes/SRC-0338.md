# SRC-0338: Barral et al. 2022 (checksum-guided zlib repair on real damage)

**Why it matters:** the only checksum-guided DEFLATE correction found, and on **real** damage
(flash bit flips, no usable ECC). It is the direct precedent for the charter's provenance grade 3
("checksum-verified bit correction, e.g. Adler-32 match"). Found late, via a citing-work check of
SRC-0308; the forensics agent also lists it.

## Method
- Estimate the bit-flip rate from two copies of encrypted flash regions, then refine it from the
  count of corrupted fragments: about one flip every 248,253 bytes (CLM-0341). This fits a Bernoulli model (supports DMG-007).
- Treat Adler-32 as a black-box oracle: generate every one-bit-flip variant of a corrupted zlib
  fragment and keep those that inflate, pass Adler-32 and have a plausible length (CLM-0340). If zlib
  stops early, only flips before that point are tried.
- A secondary oracle from the filesystem: decompressed lengths must match the inode table.
- Several survivors: either diff them by hand in a disassembler, or merge them with **three-valued
  logic**, where a bit is known only if all candidates agree (CLM-0347).

## Results
- One flip: 172 of 204 fragments have exactly one candidate (CLM-0342); 29 have none; 3 have
  several (CLM-0343).
- Two flips: 1 to 40,717 candidates per fragment (CLM-0344); 22 days to over a year per fragment on
  one machine, run on 40 machines (CLM-0345).
- Recovered data went from 8.32% to 99.68% (CLM-0348); 0.32% of bits remain indeterminate.

## Limitations and future work
- Manual disambiguation, done from scratch for every fragment (CLM-0346).
- Stream compressors (LZMA) left for future work (CLM-0349). Related work is described as thin (CLM-0339).
- The error model is bit flips; REPDF C9 substitutes whole bytes (OBS-0300).

## Transfer to PDF (our inference)
- **Directly testable on C9** (HYP-150): each damaged Flate stream has exactly one changed byte,
  so the search is at most 255 × length inflations per stream (median compressed length 9,547 bytes),
  far cheaper than their double-flip search.
- The adversary is ambiguity, not cost: Adler-32 is weak on short inputs (RFC 3309, CLM-0333..0335),
  and PDFs have many short streams. PDF offers extra oracles the paper did not have: /Length1 (font
  program length), TrueType table checksums, content-stream tokenisation, /DL.
- Three-valued merging gives a clean provenance rule: agreeing bytes are grade 3, disagreeing bytes
  are "indeterminate" and must never be emitted as known content.
