# SRC-0427: multi-task PDF extraction benchmark (Meuschke et al., 2023)

**Why it matters.** This is the most-cited recent benchmark framework for PDF text extraction. Its metric shows what
the field accepts as "correct extraction", and that standard is too lenient to detect fabrication (GAP-008).

## Setup
- **Tools.** Ten non-commercial tools: GROBID, CERMINE, Science Parse, PdfAct, PyMuPDF, Tika, Camelot, Tabula, RefExtract, Adobe Extract.
- **Content elements.** Eleven, scored on DocBank (500K arXiv pages, 1.5M annotated elements).
- **Ground truth.** DocBank labels were produced by **PDFPlumber and PDFMiner** from colour-coded LaTeX builds (CLM-0439). The ground truth therefore inherits those extractors' encoding behaviour.
- **Metrics.**
  - Token-level P/R/F1: a token counts as correct if its **Levenshtein ratio is ≥ 0.7** (CLM-0437).
  - Collated-token "accuracy", which is lenient by design (CLM-0438).
- **Results.** GROBID is best for metadata and references. Adobe Extract is best for tables (F1 0.47). No tool extracts lists; footers and equations are poor.

## Verified statements useful to W3
- Most PDF text-extraction papers do not specify their ground truth or metrics (CLM-0440, citing Mao et al.).
- Benchmarks are rarely repeated even though tools change often (CLM-0442).
- Bast and Korzen (SRC-0426) scored spurious and missing words, paragraphs and new lines for 14 tools on about 10K documents (CLM-0441). That is the finest-grained prior metric.
- Future work: diversify disciplines and document types (CLM-0443). The corpus is only born-digital LaTeX, one producer family (cf. GAP-002).

## Our reading (critique, CLM-0444)
- The ratio is LCS-based. Their Gary/Yuta example gives lev = 6 for 4+4 characters, i.e. substitution cost 2.
- So a 5-letter word with **one wrong glyph mapping** scores 0.8 and counts as correct.
- Tokens are matched order-free through a similarity matrix.
- The metric therefore cannot see single-glyph fabrication or reading-order errors. For PDFPundit it could at most serve as a coarse extractability signal, never as the fidelity endpoint.
- Candidate replacements to assess in W3:
  - flexible character accuracy (SRC-0428);
  - page-level metrics that separate recognition error from order error (SRC-0430);
  - a planted-token faithfulness test (SRC-0422 style).
