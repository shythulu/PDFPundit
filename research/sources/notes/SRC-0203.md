# SRC-0203 — Shadow attacks (standard-compliant revision tampering)

**Why it matters:** the attacks are made of exactly the structures a repair engine rebuilds or
discards: incremental updates, xref in-use/free flags, and object-number reuse. They are the best
available templates for DMG-009 and the clearest argument for GAP-015 and GAP-016.

## Attack model
The attacker prepares a *shadow document* before it is signed and makes the hidden content visible
afterwards by appending a well-formed incremental update. Three classes:
- **Hide**: overlay objects hidden later (e.g. by setting the overlay to free in a new xref).
- **Replace**: e.g. form-field appearance (`/BBox` overlay) or font replacement.
- **Hide-and-Replace**: whole shadow document inside the signed bytes. The first variant stores two objects with the same
  object number and different content (CLM-0222) and re-points the xref after signing (CLM-0223). The second variant toggles
  objects between in-use and free (CLM-0224).

## Findings (verified quotes)
- 16 of 29 viewers vulnerable (CLM-0220); attacks use only legitimate features (CLM-0221), unlike the
  earlier malformed-update ISAs (CLM-0230).
- Detection = compare the document at the first signature with the final one (CLM-0225).
- Replace cannot be prevented before signing (CLM-0228). Text selection/search may expose hidden
  content (CLM-0229).

## Limitations and future work (verified)
- PDF-Detector checked on 26 self-generated documents only (CLM-0226); more training data planned (CLM-0227).
- No real-world prevalence of shadow documents is reported.

## Implications for PDFPundit (our inference)
- A repair that re-derives one xref from all objects (REPDF-style carving) chooses *some* object per
  number; with duplicate object numbers it can surface the shadow content or erase it. Either way the
  output misrepresents the signed revision. Duplicate object numbers must be a reported tamper indicator,
  not silently resolved.
- Revision-preserving emission (append-only overlays) keeps the comparison in CLM-0225 possible.
- Generator templates for DMG-009: the three classes above, applied to known originals, plus damage on
  top (e.g., truncating the final update: DMG-008 x DMG-009).
