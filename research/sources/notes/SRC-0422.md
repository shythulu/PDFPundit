# SRC-0422: VLM OCR rewrites what it reads (FaithC4)

**Why it matters.** This is the most direct evidence on **fabrication** by learned text recognisers. It bears on
charter §2 (fabrication) and §3(c) (learned components). It also measures olmOCR-2-7B, the strongest open-weight
candidate for local PDF text recovery.

## Setup
- **Data.** 1,455 single-page PDFs rendered from mC4 text: 500 English, 500 Chinese, 455 Korean. About 5% of characters are perturbed in one of three ways: scramble, random substitution, or visually similar substitution.
- **Ground truth** is the *perturbed* text, so any "correction" is scored as an error.
- **Systems (15).**
  - General VLMs: Qwen3.5-4B, Qwen3-VL-4B, InternVL3.5-4B, Gemma4, GPT-5.4-mini, Gemini.
  - OCR-specialised VLMs: olmOCR-2-7B, DeepSeek-OCR-2, MinerU2.5-Pro, PaddleOCR-VL-1.5, LightOnOCR-2-1B.
  - Traditional OCR: Tesseract, docTR.
- **Metrics.** WER (CER for Chinese) and EDS (normalised Levenshtein similarity). Failures (refusals, repetition loops) are excluded per model.

## Results (verified)
- **WER degradation, English** (CLM-0428):
  - general VLMs: up to 6.9 pp;
  - OCR-specialised VLMs: 0.1–3.4 pp;
  - traditional OCR: <0.8 pp.
- **Rewrite rate on scrambled words** (CLM-0429):
  - Tesseract, docTR and Gemini-3-Flash: none;
  - Qwen3.5-4B: 27.35%;
  - **olmOCR-2-7B: 58.63%** (highest of all systems).
- **Non-local damage.** Errors on unperturbed words rise up to 7.3× (CLM-0430).
- **Mitigation.** A no-correction prompt cuts degradation by about 30–50% and does not remove it (CLM-0431).
- **Word length.** Rewriting affects short words (up to 10% at 4–6 characters) and drops to 0% from 8 characters (CLM-0436).
- **Authors' recommendation.** For forensics, use traditional OCR or carefully chosen OCR-VLMs (CLM-0434).

## Limitations (verified)
- Arabic and Devanagari are untested (CLM-0432).
- The perturbations are synthetic, on rendered PDFs (CLM-0433).
- Mechanistic probing covers only Qwen3-VL.

## Implications for PDFPundit (our inference)
- Any language-prior decoder is exposed to this failure. That includes REPDF's dictionary scoring, CPR's LLM check and glyph-OCR+LM, not only VLMs. GAP-201 asks for the same measurement on font-map recovery.
- **olmOCR-2-7B** is Apache-2.0 on Hugging Face (`allenai/olmOCR-2-7B-1025`, sha e52d6f09…, fetched 2026-09-27). It is the most rewrite-prone system in this study. Do not use it as a grade-1/2 text source; at most use it as a grade-6 suggestion with a flag.
- Clean benchmarks cannot see this failure (CLM-0435). The metric suite (GAP-008) needs a planted-token faithfulness test.
