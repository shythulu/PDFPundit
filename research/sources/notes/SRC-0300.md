# SRC-0300: Brown 2011, ZipRec v0.9

**Why it matters:** the founding paper on recovering DEFLATE data *after* a point of corruption.
PDF's dominant filter is Flate, so this is the base of GAP-005 and GAP-006.

## Method
1. **Find the first intact block after the damage.** DEFLATE blocks ("packets") have no signature
   and can start at any bit, so every bit offset is a candidate. Brown searches backwards from the
   end of the stream (the last-block flag is then known), rejects candidates whose Huffman-table
   header is invalid, and checks that the block ends with an end-of-block symbol exactly where the
   next block starts. Fewer than 1 in 1000 offsets survive the table check; over 80% of those that
   pass the end-of-block check are real blocks (CLM-0302).
2. **Decode with an unknown window.** The 32 KB history is filled with "unknown" markers indexed by
   position (co-indices). Back-references copy markers, so the output is literal bytes mixed with
   co-indexed unknown bytes.
3. **Reconstruct unknown bytes.** Byte-trigram constraint propagation, then a word-unigram search
   that scores candidate words against global and file-local vocabularies; greedy acceptance; optional
   iterations with decaying confidence.

## Results
- About 30% of co-indices uniquely inferred, about 75% of unknown bytes reconstructed, over 90% correct (CLM-0300).
- More iterations fill more bytes but lower accuracy, because errors propagate (CLM-0304).
- On a Real Data Corpus disk image, almost all DEFLATE streams had zero or one block (2389 vs 58 with
  two and 43 with more; CLM-0305). Block-level restart has little to work with on small streams,
  matching our OBS-0301 on PDF (64% of Flate streams are one block).

## Limitations and future work (verified quotes)
- Standard tools recover nothing after corruption (CLM-0301).
- Reconstruction works only for word-segmentable text (CLM-0303). PDF content streams, CMaps and
  font programs are not prose.
- Future work: a GUI where one human correction updates every co-indexed occurrence (CLM-0306).
  This is a neat model for exception-only escalation: one decision fixes many bytes.

## Our reading (inference, not evidence)
- Every reconstructed byte is an inference (grade 4 or 6 in charter terms), not recovery. The
  paper's own colour-coded confidence display is a precedent for per-byte provenance.
- The method needs the corruption point to be known or the stream start to be missing. C9-style
  scattered substitutions are neither (see SRC-0301 and OBS-0300).
- **Testable on PDF:** yes. Unknown-window decoding is simple to implement; see HYP-151.
